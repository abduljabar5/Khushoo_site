Support

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Focus Mode blocking work?

Khushoo uses Apple's Screen Time API to block apps you select during prayer times. When prayer time begins, the selected apps will show a shield screen instead of opening. You can unlock early after 5 minutes or wait for the full duration.

Why do I need to grant Screen Time permission?

Screen Time permission is required for the Focus Mode feature to work. This allows Khushoo to temporarily restrict access to apps you've selected during prayer times. Your Screen Time data stays on your device and is never sent to us.

Can I customize which prayers trigger blocking?

Yes! You can enable or disable blocking for each of the 5 daily prayers (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha) individually in the Focus settings.

What is Strict Mode?

Strict Mode adds an extra layer of accountability. When enabled, you must confirm that you've prayed before apps are unlocked, helping you stay committed to your prayer practice.

How are prayer times calculated?

Prayer times are calculated using your location and the calculation method you select in settings. We support multiple calculation methods used worldwide.

How do I restore my purchases?

Go to Profile > Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.
